Privacy Policy

Media Widget · Last updated 25 September 2026

In short: Media Widget does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything the app reads stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party data sharing.

Media Widget (“the app”) is a home-screen widget that shows what is currently playing on your device and lets you control playback. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What the app accesses

To show the current track and provide playback controls, the app uses Android’s Notification Access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE) and the system Media Session APIs. Through these, the app reads — only while it is running on your device:

  • The title, artist, and album of the media currently playing.
  • The album artwork provided by the playing app.
  • Playback position and state (playing/paused), to draw the progress bar.
  • The identity of the app that is playing, so you can pick which sources to show.

This information is used only to render the widget and to send playback commands (play, pause, next, previous) back to the app you are listening to.

What the app does with it

  • All processing happens locally on your device.
  • Album artwork may be cached temporarily in the app’s private storage so the widget can redraw quickly. This cache is never uploaded anywhere and is removed when the app’s data is cleared.
  • Your widget settings (such as which sources to show and appearance options) are stored locally on your device.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not send any of the above information off your device.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • It does not create an account or ask for your identity.
  • It does not read the contents of your notifications beyond the media playback information described above.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Permissions

  • Notification Access — required to detect currently-playing media and to control playback. You grant this manually in Android Settings and can revoke it at any time.
  • Ignore Battery Optimizations (if present) — requested only to improve the reliability of live updates on devices that aggressively suspend background apps. It transmits no data.

Children’s privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
